## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **GIANTESS PLAYGROUND**. This trainer enhances the destruction sandbox with optional protection, time, score, growth, movement, and spawning controls for Sandbox, Rampage, Hide and Seek, and photo sessions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want more control over city destruction, Rampage Mode timers and scores, giantess growth, tiny-citizen survival, sandbox spawning, exploration, and staged photo sessions.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Rampage Time** — Stops the Rampage Mode countdown so you can continue destroying the city without running out of time.
- **Maximum Rampage Score** — Locks the current score at a high value for uninterrupted score-based destruction sessions.
- **Instant Giant Growth** — Applies rapid growth during Rampage Mode so destruction increases the giantess scale more quickly.
- **Giantess Protection** — Reduces unwanted failure conditions while controlling a giantess during destruction and sandbox activities.
- **Photo Session Control** — Supports controlled posing and scene preparation for destruction-themed photo sessions with characters and destructible props.

### 📌 Survival

- **Tiny Citizen Protection** — Prevents the controlled tiny citizen from being crushed while navigating the city and avoiding giantess footsteps.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Free Movement** — Enables enhanced movement control for exploring urban environments from either playable perspective.

### 🌍 World

- **Sandbox Spawn Assist** — Provides convenient access to sandbox spawning controls for creating vehicles, tanks, citizens, and other interactive scene elements.
- **Hide and Seek Assistance** — Improves visibility and survivability during Hide and Seek hunts involving giantess characters and tiny citizens.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the trainer after launching GIANTESS PLAYGROUND and select the desired movement option. Enhanced movement is useful when exploring large urban maps as a giantess or maneuvering between buildings and feet as a tiny citizen. Disable the option before changing modes if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

GIANTESS PLAYGROUND does not use a conventional character inventory system. The trainer therefore focuses on sandbox scene controls rather than item duplication or equipment editing. Use the game's own menu to select characters, animations, spawned objects, and other available scene elements.

### World & Environment

World options are intended for the game's destructible city environments. Sandbox Spawn Assist can help prepare scenes containing citizens, vehicles, tanks, buildings, and other interactive objects. Use protection options when experimenting with large destruction chains or when setting up photo sessions.

### Combat & Weapons

The game is centered on physical destruction and survival rather than a traditional weapon-based combat system. Soldiers, tanks, vehicles, buildings, and citizens function as interactive targets or hazards. Use Rampage controls for timed destruction challenges and Tiny Citizen Protection when attempting to evade giantess attacks.
